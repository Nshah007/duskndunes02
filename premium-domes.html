Premium Desert Domes in Jaisalmer

A MORE PRIVATE STAY

Seven premium domes, set apart on the dunes

For guests who want a quieter, more secluded stay, Dusk N Dunes offers 7 premium domes set apart from the main tent rows. The dome format opens up wide, uninterrupted views of the desert around the camp, making it a favourite for a more intimate stay at Sam Sand Dunes.

The domes carry the same premium desert experience as the rest of the camp — comfort, privacy, and direct access to the dunes — in a distinct architectural style that stands apart from the traditional tent.

Like all guests at Dusk N Dunes, dome guests have full access to the restaurant, cultural evenings and desert activities.
