WELCOME TO DUSK N DUNES

A premium desert camp on the edge of the Sam Sand Dunes

Set against the open horizon of the Thar Desert, Dusk N Dunes is a premium desert camp at Sam Sand Dunes, just outside Jaisalmer. It is a place built for travellers who want the desert without giving up comfort — where golden dunes meet thoughtfully appointed Swiss tents and premium domes.

Every stay here is layered with Rajasthani hospitality: warm welcomes, home-style multi-cuisine dining, folk music around the fire and a calm that only the open desert can offer. By day, the dunes invite adventure — jeep safaris, camel rides and quad biking across the sand. By evening, the camp turns inward, with cultural performances, candlelight dinners on the dunes and a sky full of stars.

Whether you are visiting Jaisalmer for a short desert escape or planning a longer Thar Desert experience, Dusk N Dunes is designed to be an unhurried, memorable base at one of Rajasthan's most photographed landscapes.
