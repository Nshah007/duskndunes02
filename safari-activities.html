SAM SAND DUNES SAFARI

Jeep Safari & Camel Safari in Jaisalmer

Sam Sand Dunes is one of the most well-known spots for a desert safari in Jaisalmer. Guests at Dusk N Dunes can arrange a jeep safari or camel safari directly through the camp, with timings built around sunrise, sunset or a full-day desert outing.

STAR GAZING

Desert skies, far from city lights

Once the desert cools down and the camp lights dim, the sky over Sam Sand Dunes opens up. Star gazing is one of the quieter highlights of a stay at Dusk N Dunes — no telescope required, just an open patch of sand and a clear Thar Desert night.
